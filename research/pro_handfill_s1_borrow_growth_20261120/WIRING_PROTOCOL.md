# Exploratory extension registered before evaluation

Can the new threshold unit learn its connections when all old scratch-board columns are available? Hold the adder fixed. Supply the 18 old board bits and the new unit's previous predicted borrow through a forecast feedback register, giving 19 inputs plus a bias. Do not select three operands in advance. For each of 16 NumPy seeds 20261120 through 20261135, draw 64 distinct ordered pairs from either the full byte range or both operands in 0..15. Each pair contributes eight teacher-forced local examples (previous true borrow available during training). Use a zero-initialized integer perceptron, shuffled epochs, up to 64 epochs, stop on zero errors over the full training table. Evaluate every ordered byte pair for all eight steps, feeding back the model's own prediction. Retain all seeds, updates, training fit, order-sensitive accuracy and nonzero connections outside A0, B0 and borrow. This is an exploratory follow-up with a manually provided unit and register, not open-ended growth.
