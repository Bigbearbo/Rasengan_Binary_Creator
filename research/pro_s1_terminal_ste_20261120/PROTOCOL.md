# Frozen S1, terminal-only supervision and straight-through gradients

Before any run, compare identity STE, clipped STE (|z| <= 1), literal zero gradient, and identity STE with temporal gradient detached. A newly supplied hard threshold cell reads A0, B0 and its own prediction from the previous one of eight rounds of the frozen upstream serial adder, with zero initial recurrent state and all four new parameters zero. The only training label is whether the first whole unsigned byte is less than the second. No local or mid-sequence teacher. Each of 16 paired NumPy seeds (20261120..20261135) provides 64 distinct uniform ordered byte pairs. Full batch SGD with learning rate 0.25 for exactly 256 steps and a terminal half squared error. All four gradient arms use identical forward and evaluation conditions. Also exhaustively evaluate the training terminal loss on all 81 vectors in {-1,0,1}^4, breaking ties by sparsity then lexicographic order. This independently supplies a small non-gradient reference; effort is accounted separately.

For every final model count terminal success and entire eight-step borrow sequences on the exhaustive domain (65,536 inputs). Compare raw floating hard forecasts, direct rounding, and optional small integer equivalents limited to [-2,2]^4 by matching the floating cell's eight local outputs, without consulting ground-truth labels. Report freezes, actual weight changes, rounded/equivalent deployment and original S1 arithmetic regression. Distinguish feasibility on a known four-parameter topology from discovering a topology or learning a larger recurrent circuit.
