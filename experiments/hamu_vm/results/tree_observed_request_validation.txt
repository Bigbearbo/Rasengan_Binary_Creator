Limits: kernel still has one uniform op_order across every program window; no variable per-word width implemented. Mask comes from the guest work area after the current-context guard. The host validates all four fresh request outputs and executes the selected bit. Elimination remains monotone; if a selected fragment repeatedly fails execution, the first-survivor policy can still stall. Prediction steps are reported, not charged to HP. No unsandboxed purity claim or broader autonomous evolution claim follows from these tests.
